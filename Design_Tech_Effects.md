# Tech Effects & Performance Visualization Guide (Design_Tech_Effects.md)

This styling manual defines the physical parameters, emission properties, spectral colors, and optical bloom standards for the **Tech Effects Mode** in the Elmich AI application.

---

## 1. High-Tech Performance Visualization Philosophy

The Tech Effects Mode is designed to convert static home appliance images into dynamic engineering diagrams, visually explaining proprietary technology (e.g., a air fryer’s 3D hot air cyclone circulation, an induction cooktop's electromagnetic waves, or an evaporative fan's cooling air stream):
- **Structural Integrity:** The home appliance remains the absolute physical anchor of the frame, preserving 100% of its spatial configuration. The visual effects must originate from inside the appliance or circulate around it in accordance with physical and mechanics logic.
- **Sleek Minimalist Aesthetic:** Glowing emission lines or energy vectors must be thin, precise, and elegant. Avoid overly dense, chaotic, or distracting effects. Prioritize clean, sharp vector pathways that contour the appliance.

---

## 2. Technical Specializations for Key Technologies

### 2.1. Thermal & Heating Circulation Loops (Heating Physics)
- **Visual Forms:** Flowing electromagnetic copper lines under glass plates, cylindrical heating waves, or 3D tornado heat pathways (3D Cyclone/Vortex path).
- **Color Grading:** Smooth gradients transitioning from deep infrared crimson to glowing orange and bright yellow, utilizing a soft glowing ambiance (`subtle neon glow`) that reflects warm tones onto adjacent metallic walls.
- **Engineering Prompt:** `"Illustrate precise volumetric heat circulation pathways using flowing golden-orange vector lines. Apply a soft ambient glow on internal oven walls that indicates active heating cycle."`

### 2.2. Cooling Currents & Vapor Dispersion (Cooling Physics)
- **Visual Forms:** Crystal-clear ocean current waves wrapping the appliance, a thin layer of dense cool mist sweeping the ground area, or microscopic bubble trajectories floating upward.
- **Color Grading:** Elegant sapphire blue (`Cobalt Blue`), pale emerald (`Soft Cyan`), and pure frosted silver light highlights.
- **Engineering Prompt:** `"Render frozen arctic ocean current waves seamlessly circulating the product base. Integrate a smooth transition from deep navy blue to crystal clear ice turquoise, decorated with gentle cool fog dispersion."`

### 2.3. Original Markings Removal (Clean Engineering Showcase)
- **Requirement:** Fully erase messy hand-drawn annotation marks, rough prototyping sketches, or test signatures from the source photo. Reconstruct pristine, clean matte polymer or brushed metal casing surfaces, optimizing the model for clean catalog placement.

---

## 3. Optical Bloom & Reflection Physics (Optics & Blooming Rules)

- **Specular Bloom Control:**
  - Glowing effects must not overexpose (clip) or cover up the appliance casing, control switches, or the Elmich logo.
  - The heat or cold glowing pathways must cast a subtle, soft color reflection back onto adjacent metal rims and corners (`specular bloom bounce`).
- **Premium Dark High-Tech Staging:**
  - Frequently stage these effects against a dark, moody background. Incorporate a reflective, dark ground plane to catch soft, mirror-like reflections of the glowing energy loops (`subtle dark floor reflections`), enhancing spatial depth.

---

## 4. Defect Prevention Checklist (Negative Guidelines)

- **Overdesigned Sci-Fi Shrapnel (Sci-Fi Slop):** Avoid chaotic lightning bolts, generic Sci-Fi or magical shields, or fragmented sparks that resemble fantasy action scenes rather than premium kitchen appliance engineering.
- **Misaligned Airflows (Physical Clipping):** Airflows, flames, or cooling mist must not leak through solid metal walls in unrealistic ways.
- **Floating Light Source Shadowing (Light Leakage Errors):** Emitted glowing light rays must not cast sharp, awkward self-shadows onto the floor. Ensure the glowing pathways behave purely as emission sources.
