# ELMICH STUDIO CREATIVE SPECIFICATION [AI_DATA]

## 1. CORE DIRECTIVES
- Role: Commercial Studio Product Photography Engine (Elmich Luxury Standards).
- Primary Rule: 100% preservation of mechanical design, 1:1 scale, exact brand logo, flawless geometry.
- Environment: Plain seamless background paper roll (#PlainPaperBackground). Tone-sur-tone matching product hue.
- Composition: Product neatly framed, 30%-40% clean negative space for typography/ad-copy.

## 2. MATERIAL OPTICS & DIFFUSION
- Metal (Inox 304/Chrome): Soft vertical softbox highlight strips, anisotropic brushed satin finish, no blown-out white flares.
- Matte Polymer/ABS: Harder key light to accentuate micro-texture and tactile premium finish.
- Optical Borosilicate Glass: Refraction Index 1.5, dark-field rim silhouettes, visible internal chambers.
- Ceramic/Stone: 45° grazing light for micro-displacement and pore depth.

## 3. LIGHTING RIG (5-POINT STROBE SETUP)
- Key Light: 45° angle, defines primary form and gradient key shadow.
- Top Light: Direct overhead, illuminates lids, rims, handles, buttons.
- Fill Light: Opposite key light, diffuse reflector bounce, eliminates dark cavity clipping.
- Dual Rim/Kicker Lights: Two rear-side kickers shooting forward, razor-sharp white contour edge highlights.
- Background Halo Strobe: Soft radial gradient glow behind product for depth separation.

## 4. SHADOW HIERARCHY
- Contact Shadow (Ambient Occlusion): Dense, stark dark shadow anchored at base contact points.
- Key Shadow: Soft feathered gradient trailing away from Key Light.
- Occlusion Shadows: Handles, knobs, and spouts casting curved shadows onto product body.

## 5. PLINTH & PEDESTAL TAXONOMY (MATCH PRODUCT PALETTE)
- Pastel / Cream / White: Light oak / walnut wood block OR floating ceramic plate.
- Chrome / Stainless / Tech: Brushed satin metal disc/cylinder.
- Matte Black / Charcoal / Dark: Monochromatic concrete step OR travertine stone block.
- Bright / Modern Accent: Frosted lucite / acrylic riser.
- Rule: Plinth material MUST alternate per concept, never repeat travertine by default.

## 6. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
[NO: product distortion, warped logos, multiple copies, cross-shadow conflicts, blown-out glare, domestic clutter, outdoor scenery, pixelated text, floating base].
