# ELMICH & VIETNAMESE 3D PACKAGING MOCKUP ENGINE SPECIFICATION [AI_DATA]

## 1. CORE DIRECTIVES & GEOMETRIC FOLDING ENGINE
- **Role**: Precision 3D Commercial Packaging Folding & Rendering Engine for Vietnamese Consumer Goods & Gift Packaging.
- **Dieline Flat Layout Mapping**: Deconstruct the flat unfolded 2D dieline into its physical 3D box panels:
  - **Front Panel (Mặt Trước)**: Main marketing artwork (hero product photo, primary headline, key brand message).
  - **Back Panel (Mặt Sau)**: Secondary artwork (product appliance, features, secondary branding, certification marks).
  - **Side Panels (2 Mặt Hông)**: Left and Right panels (solid brand background color, secondary brand logos, side specs).
  - **Top Lid (Mặt Nắp Trên)**: Top lid panel folded horizontally at 90 degrees, containing die-cut handle slots. The text and logos on the top lid must orient upright and readable from the camera's elevated perspective (never rendered upside down).
  - **Bottom & Glue Flaps (Đáy & Tai Dán)**: Folded inward and completely hidden from view.
- **Perspective Fidelity**: Graphic elements, barcodes, badges, and typography conform strictly to 3-point perspective lines without geometric distortion.
- **Typographic Accuracy**: 100% preservation of Vietnamese diacritics, brand logos, headings, and legal text without gibberish or blur.

## 2. VIETNAMESE PACKAGING PLASTIC HANDLE SPECIFICATION (QUAI NHỰA TRONG DẺO)
- **Plastic Handle Geometry**: A flexible, curved arch handle (quai nhựa xách bán nguyệt dẻo) anchored into two standard die-cut rectangular slots positioned symmetrically on the top lid.
- **Translucent Plastic Material**: Semi-clear, frosted translucent polyethylene/polypropylene material (`translucent milky-clear flexible molded plastic`). Captures soft ambient light refraction, subtle white specular rim highlights along the curved ridge, and cast micro-shadows directly onto the top lid beneath the handle arch.
- **Handle Fastening**: Two flat rectangular T-ends or anchor studs securely clipped into the two die-cut slots on the top lid, flush against the surface.

## 3. STAGING MODES & DUAL-BOX COMPOSITION
### 3.1. Dual Mockup (Front & Back Side-by-Side — Standard Display)
- **Arrangement**: Exactly TWO (2) identical 3D packaging boxes placed side-by-side on an absolute pure white commercial studio floor and backdrop (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Left Box (3/4 Front Angle)**: Turned at a 30°-35° angle to prominently display the **Front Panel**, the **Right Side Panel**, and the **Top Lid with the clear plastic handle**.
  - **Right Box (3/4 Back Angle)**: Turned at an angle to prominently display the **Back Panel**, the **Right Side Panel**, and the **Top Lid with the clear plastic handle**.
- **Visual Balance**: Both boxes stand at the identical scale and horizon line, parallel with balanced spacing, demonstrating the complete front and back designs in a single unified commercial presentation.
### 3.2. Single Angle View
- One box centered at an elegant 3/4 perspective angle showing Front (or Back), Side, and Top with handle.

## 4. MECHANICAL FOLD & MATERIAL TEXTURE SPECIFICATIONS
- **Paper Crease Highlights**: Folded edges must exhibit realistic paperboard thickness (1.2mm - 1.5mm micro-radiused crease), catching clean, crisp studio softbox specular highlights along folded boundaries.
- **Paperboard Substrates**:
  - *Ivory / Duplex Coated (Hộp bồi sóng E / Ivory bóng mịn)*: Smooth coated surface with elegant satin luster, deep color saturation, sharp ink contrast.
  - *Kraft Corrugated (Carton sóng nâu)*: Natural fibrous cellulose texture, matte ink absorption, visible fluting micro-ridges.
- **Grounding & Studio Shadows**: Crisp, dark contact drop shadows grounding the bottom edges of the boxes, diffusing softly into a pure seamless `#FFFFFF` background. No floating box artifacts.
